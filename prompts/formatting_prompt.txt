5. CLEAN SPACING:
   - Clean up double spaces, strange paragraph line breaks, or carriage returns. Ensure each block element is separated by exactly one blank line.
6. STRICT PROHIBITIONS:
   - DO NOT summarize any content.
   - DO NOT delete, shorten, or simplify any educational text.
